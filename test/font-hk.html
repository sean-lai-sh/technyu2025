Weight note (HK Grotesque vs Satoshi): HK Grotesque 400 Regular has a mechanically lighter stroke than Satoshi 400 at the same size — especially visible in the display-1/2 range. The letterforms are more open, neutral, and geometric. At heading-1/2 (500 Medium), the weight difference narrows. HK has no 900 Black weight; maximum is 800 ExtraBold. Satoshi's 400 reads as slightly denser and more confident at mid-sizes.
