Our Approach

Three pillars,
one progression.

Step 01

Find Your Role

A tight cohort for figuring out where you fit. Workshops, company visits, peer pressure that actually compounds.

Outcome

A clearer sense of direction and a first step into a more specific path.

Step 02

Hone Your Role

One-on-one pairings with mentors from Google, Meta, Databricks, and beyond.

Outcome

Stronger judgment, better framing, and a more intentional path forward.

Step 03

Practice Your Role

Startup-style pods shipping production code to real users with real accountability.

Outcome

Practical experience, shipped work, and a better sense of how to operate on a team.
